Q. What happens to the mass?
Why no change?

① Atoms are never created or destroyed

② The kinds and numbers of atoms are the same before and after

→ A chemical reaction only changes how atoms combine!

Example calculation
sulfuric acid 10 g + barium hydroxide solution 17 g
→ barium sulfate 23 g + water 4 g
Before 27 g = After 27 g
Quick recap of the 4 experiments

⬆ Up burn in open air → oxygen enters

➡ Same sealed → nothing in or out

⬇ Down open + gas forms → gas leaves

In every case atoms just rearrange. Only what crosses the boundary (the air) changes the reading.
